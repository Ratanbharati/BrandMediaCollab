BRAND MEDIA COLLAB · PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 November 2026

Publication note: This working draft describes the current product and intended data flows. Before public launch, confirm the legal operator and contact details, retention periods, vendors, and user-rights process with the platform operator and local privacy counsel.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy explains how Brand Media Collab (“BMC”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use its website, create a member account, publish a profile or opportunity, contact another member, or connect a social account. BMC is currently a free discovery and communication platform; we do not collect collaboration payments or charge a commission.

2. Information we handle

  • Account details: name, email, account type, authentication identifiers, and account settings.
  • Profile and professional details: biography, organization, location, services, portfolio, social links, profile image, and visibility setting.
  • Platform activity: projects and briefs, applications or expressions of interest, saved items, connections, messages, support requests, and safety reports.
  • Connected social data: if you authorize Instagram or YouTube, the platform may provide your account identifier, public channel/profile details, audience totals, and available account or content insights. The exact fields depend on the permissions and API access granted by that platform.
  • Technical information: basic request, device, and security logs generated by the website, hosting, authentication, and database services.

3. How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts; show profiles and opportunities according to visibility settings; support search and discovery; deliver messages and relevant service notifications; provide connected-account metrics to the account owner; respond to support or abuse reports; maintain, troubleshoot, and improve BMC; and meet legal obligations.

4. Public and private information

Profile and project information you publish may be visible to other members, especially while discoverability is enabled. Your messages are intended for their conversation participants. Private contact details and social OAuth credentials are not intended for public profile display. BMC stores social access tokens encrypted on the server; they are not returned to the browser. Detailed account insights are shown to the connected account owner, while selected follower/subscriber totals and social profile links may be used in discovery when your profile is visible.

5. Social account connections

Connecting is optional. You authorize Meta/Instagram or Google/YouTube directly and can disconnect through Settings & Privacy. BMC does not collect your social password. When disconnected, BMC removes the stored authorization and connected metrics from its active social connection records; platform-side authorization may need to be revoked separately in your Meta or Google account settings. The social provider may independently process your data under its own policy.

6. Sharing and service providers

We use service providers needed to run the product, including database/authentication and website hosting providers (currently Supabase and Vercel). We send information to Meta or Google only as needed for a connection you initiate and authorized API requests. We may disclose information when required by law, to address security or abuse, or to protect members and the service. BMC does not sell member personal information.

7. Storage, security, and retention

We use access controls and security measures intended to protect information, including database permissions and server-side encryption for social authorization tokens. No online system can be guaranteed completely secure. We retain information while needed to provide the service, maintain account and safety records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Exact retention periods should be confirmed and published by the operator before launch.

8. Your choices and requests

You can edit your profile, adjust discoverability, disconnect social accounts, and download certain account data through available account settings. You may contact support to ask about access, correction, deletion, or other privacy requests. We may need to verify your identity and may retain information where law permits or requires it. Some information shared with another member may already be visible to that member or included in their records.

9. Children

BMC is intended for professional use by adults and is not designed for children. Do not create an account if you are not legally able to enter into the relevant agreement.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as features, providers, or legal requirements change. We will publish the updated version here and revise its effective date. For privacy questions or requests, use Help & Support. The operator should add a verified legal identity and dedicated privacy contact before public launch.
