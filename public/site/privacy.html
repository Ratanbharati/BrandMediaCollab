BRAND MEDIA COLLAB · PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 November 2026

Publication note: This working draft describes the current product and intended data flows. Before public launch, confirm the legal operator and contact details, retention periods, vendors, and user-rights process with the platform operator and local privacy counsel.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy explains how Brand Media Collab (“BMC”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use its website, create a member account, publish a profile or opportunity, add public profile links, or contact another member. BMC is currently a free discovery and communication platform; we do not collect collaboration payments or charge a commission.

2. Information we handle

  • Account details: name, email, account type, authentication identifiers, and account settings.
  • Profile and professional details: biography, organization, location, services, portfolio, social links, profile image, and visibility setting.
  • Platform activity: projects and briefs, applications or expressions of interest, saved items, connections, messages, support requests, and safety reports.
  • Public profile links: website and social URLs that you choose to enter, such as Instagram or YouTube profile links.
  • Technical information: basic request, device, and security logs generated by the website, hosting, authentication, and database services.

3. How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts; show profiles and opportunities according to visibility settings; support search and discovery; deliver messages and relevant service notifications; provide connected-account metrics to the account owner; respond to support or abuse reports; maintain, troubleshoot, and improve BMC; and meet legal obligations.

4. Public and private information

Profile and project information you publish may be visible to other members, especially while discoverability is enabled. Your messages are intended for their conversation participants. Website and social URLs you add to your profile may be shown to other signed-in members when your profile is discoverable. BMC does not collect social account passwords, authorization tokens, follower totals, or account analytics in this link-only feature.

5. Social profile links

Adding a public social link is optional. BMC stores the URL you submit so it can display that link on your profile. BMC does not sign in to the social account, verify that you own it, or retrieve profile metrics or insights. You can edit or remove a link from your profile settings. When you open an external link, that provider may process information under its own privacy policy.

6. Sharing and service providers

We use service providers needed to run the product, including database/authentication and website hosting providers (currently Supabase and Vercel). BMC does not send profile link data to Meta or Google to retrieve account information. We may disclose information when required by law, to address security or abuse, or to protect members and the service. BMC does not sell member personal information.

7. Storage, security, and retention

We use access controls and security measures intended to protect account and profile information, including database permissions that limit who can manage saved profile links. No online system can be guaranteed completely secure. We retain information while needed to provide the service, maintain account and safety records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Exact retention periods should be confirmed and published by the operator before launch.

8. Your choices and requests

You can edit or remove profile links, adjust discoverability, and download certain account data through available account settings. You may contact support to ask about access, correction, deletion, or other privacy requests. We may need to verify your identity and may retain information where law permits or requires it. Some information shared with another member may already be visible to that member or included in their records.

9. Children

BMC is intended for professional use by adults and is not designed for children. Do not create an account if you are not legally able to enter into the relevant agreement.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as features, providers, or legal requirements change. We will publish the updated version here and revise its effective date. For privacy questions or requests, use Help & Support. The operator should add a verified legal identity and dedicated privacy contact before public launch.
